Offline sales sync to the server

Queued sales keep their offline number. Stock moves on the device. VAT and totals are checked again on sync.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Sales queue on the device and sync when the network returns.

How are refunds handled?

As credit notes linked to the original invoice.

Can staff have different permissions?

Yes. Owners set what cashiers may refund, discount or edit.

What currency does it use?

Sudanese pounds (SDG), with VAT on each invoice.
